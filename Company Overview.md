# Eternal Private Limited

Eternal Limited is an Indian technology led consumer business group operating across food delivery, quick commerce, going-out experiences and restaurant supply.
It was formerly known as Zomato Limited. The corporate name changed to Eternal in 2025 as the company expanded beyond its original food delivery identity. Its stock exchange symbol is now ETERNAL.

Eternal is a parent company for the listed four companies that expand its business into food,commerce,lifestyle and supply.

Zomato = its food delivery business.
Blinkit = its quick commerce business.
District = its lifestyle business.
Hyperpure = its B2B restaurant supply business.

The transition from Zomato Limited to Eternal Limited in 2025 represented a broader corporate identity for a business whose operations had expanded beyond food delivery.

Rather than functioning as a single purpose food delivery platform, Eternal now participates in multiple consumer spending categories, including food, groceries, everyday essentials, entertainment and restaurant supply chains.

The company's stated mission is to power India's changing lifestyles.

## History Of Eternal's Business Expansion 

Stage 1 – Food discovery and delivery

The company's original business centred on helping consumers discover restaurants and access food-related information. It subsequently expanded into online food ordering and delivery.

Stage 2 – Expansion into restaurant supply chains

With the development of Hyperpure in 2019, the company expanded into B2B supplies, connecting restaurant businesses with food ingredients and related products.

This represented an expansion beyond consumer-facing transactions into the restaurant supply ecosystem.

Stage 3 – Entry into quick commerce

The company entered quick commerce through Blinkit, which was acquired in August 2022.

This acquisition enabled Eternal to participate in the rapidly developing market for on-demand delivery of groceries and other everyday products.

Stage 4 – Diversification into going-out experiences

District was launched in 2022 to address consumer demand for dining-out, movies, sporting events and live entertainment.

This expanded the company's presence into experience-based consumption.

Stage 5 – Corporate transformation

In 2025, Zomato Limited adopted the name Eternal Limited, reflecting the evolution of its business portfolio.

By FY2026, the company had developed into a multi-business consumer technology group with operations across four principal categories.
