# 2. Understanding Eternal's Economic System

## 2.1 Food Delivery Industry : Economical And Financial Structure

What industry is Zomato actually operating in?

Zomato's food-delivery business operates in the digital food-delivery platform industry. It connects three distinct participant groups:
- Consumers — demand food.
- Restaurants — supply prepared food.
- Delivery partners — fulfil orders by transporting food.

A conventional business generally sells a product or service directly to a customer. A platform business creates value by facilitating interactions between different groups.

A multi-sided market (or multi-sided platform) is an economic model where a platform or intermediary connects two or more distinct, interdependent user groups and creates value by facilitating interactions between them.
